Common questions
Why was I charged for a visit?
Included visits are never charged. Extra visits use your member rate, and the payment page tells you why.
Can I cancel a visit?
Yes, up to 12 hours before. Included visits return to your allowance.
What if my video fails?
Rejoin from My visits, or use the backup link we email you.
Is this for emergencies?
No. In an emergency call your local emergency number.
