Telehealth Consent

What you agree to when you meet by video.

1. What telehealth is

Receiving care by secure video and messaging rather than in person.

2. Benefits and limits

Convenient and private, but physical examination is not possible and technology can fail. A backup video link is provided.

3. Your location

Rules depend on where you are during the visit. You will confirm your location each time you book.

4. Privacy and recording

Visits are private and are not recorded. Please join from a quiet place where you can speak freely.

5. Emergencies

Telehealth is not for emergencies. Call 911 in the US or 112 in Nigeria.

6. Your right to stop

You can withdraw consent or choose in-person care at any time.
