Rules the screens follow
A visit that is included in the plan is never charged. The patient never sees a payment step.
The allowance is reserved when you book and returns if you cancel more than 12 hours ahead.
Visits beyond the allowance, or types the plan does not include, are charged at the member rate.
Every payment screen says why the amount is what it is.
Nigerian cards pay in naira at a fixed monthly price.
